02 · SPACE SCIENCE / TIME SERIES

Aditya-L1 solar flare forecasting

Can X-ray measurements give us an early signal of solar flare activity?

Used SoLEXS and HEL1OS X-ray data to build classification and regression models, engineer temporal features, and validate them with time-aware splits. The goal was to forecast activity up to 30 minutes ahead.

97%True positive rate
98%Precision
30.34MAE
67.61RMSE

Metrics reflect the evaluated dataset and task; MAE/RMSE are regression metrics.
